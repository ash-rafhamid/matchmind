# MatchMind - 2-minute demo video script

Target length **1:50** (the limit is 2:00 - leave room). Match used: **Redmond Rovers vs Ironvale Athletic** (3-3).
Each scene: what to show, what to click, and the voice-over (short sentences, easy to read aloud).

## Before you record

1. Open https://matchmind.blackisland-4799a434.japaneast.azurecontainerapps.io/ a minute before, so the server is
   awake (or set Min replicas to 1 first).
2. Chrome or Edge, full screen (F11), 1920x1080 if possible. Close other tabs and notifications.
3. Turn sound on - the stadium and the commentator are part of the demo.
4. Click around once (Play, a story, the language menu) so everything is loaded before the real take.
5. Language **English**, audience **Casual fan** to start.

**Free tools on Windows 11:** **Clipchamp** (already installed - search "Clipchamp" in Start) records the screen and
your voice, and lets you cut and add captions. Or press **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar) to record the browser.
Upload to YouTube as **Public**.

## Scene 1 - The hook (0:00-0:10)

**Show:** the home page. The trailer is playing a goal: build-up, goal cam, celebration. Don't touch anything.

**Voice-over:**
> This is MatchMind - an AI match crew that watches every touch, calls it live, and explains why it happened.

## Scene 2 - The data (0:10-0:18)

**Show:** scroll down slowly to **Fixtures**, then back up. Click **Watch with the crew →**.

**Voice-over:**
> Every match is synthetic: a calibrated simulator and ten-frames-a-second tracking, built for Premier League Studio.

## Scene 3 - The broadcast (0:18-0:45)

**Show:** on the match page click **Highlights**, then **▶ Play**. Let it run: the commentator's voice, a goal, the
GOAL graphic, the celebration, the slow-motion replay, and the crew's story appearing at the bottom with
**Fact-checked**. If you have time, click one camera button (e.g. **Tactical**) to show the cut.

**Voice-over** (keep it quiet so the commentator is heard):
> A live commentator, in an Azure neural voice.
> The AI director cuts the cameras and replays the goal.
> And the crew explains the moment - for you.

## Scene 4 - The crew (0:45-1:05)

**Show:** pause. In the right panel (**Live**), under a goal story, click **How the crew did it**. Scroll the steps
slowly: Producer, Analyst and Tactician, Storyteller, Fact-checker - **Passed**.

**Voice-over:**
> Behind every story is a Microsoft Agent Framework crew on Microsoft Foundry.
> The Analyst and the Tactician work in parallel, the Storyteller writes,
> and a fact-checker - plain code - checks every number, name and score.
> Wrong drafts go back. Nothing unverified goes on air.

## Scene 5 - Ask and What-if (1:05-1:25)

**Show:** close the trace. Click the **Ask** tab, then the suggested question **Who was the best player, and why?**
Let the answer appear. Then click **What if**: set the minute to **60** (it starts at the minute you paused on), move
**Pressing** towards *hunt the ball*, click **Simulate**, and let the win-chance result and **The Tactician's
reading** appear (about 10 seconds - you can cut the wait in the edit).

**Voice-over:**
> Ask the crew anything - the Host agent looks it up through our MCP server.
> Or test a tactic: the What-if Lab replays the rest of the match hundreds of times
> and tells you honestly if it would have helped.

## Scene 6 - Every fan, every language (1:25-1:40)

**Show:** open the language menu (top right). First choose **Young fan (8-12)**, then **Español** (after that the
menu itself is in Spanish). The whole site and the story switch. Click the **Live** tab, press **▶ Play** for a few seconds so the Spanish commentator is heard.

**Voice-over:**
> Five audiences, ten languages - even audio description for blind fans.

## Scene 7 - Close (1:40-1:50)

**Show:** go back to the home page (click **MatchMind** top left). Trailer playing. Optionally show the link as a
caption.

**Voice-over:**
> Built on Microsoft Foundry, Agent Framework, Azure AI Speech, MCP and Azure Container Apps - with no keys in
> production. MatchMind: every match, explained.
